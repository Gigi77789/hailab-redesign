NEU HAI noun, /nuː haɪ/

  1. The Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence Lab at Northeastern University, led by Prof. Dakuo Wang and based in the Khoury College of Computer Sciences and the College of Arts, Media and Design.
  2. A way of building AI with the people who will use it: study how clinicians, teachers, families, and everyday users work, design systems around that work, and test them where it happens.
