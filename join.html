Join us

We work on educational AI, health care AI, and LLM agents, often on NSF, NIH, and industry-sponsored projects. If that’s your kind of problem, we’d like to hear from you.

Fill in the application form d.wang@northeastern.edu

  • PhD students

    Apply through Northeastern’s official application. We contact candidates for Zoom interviews around February and March.

    You don’t need to fill in the form unless you have more to tell us.

  • Postdocs, visiting scholars, and research scientists

    If you hold a doctoral degree and work on educational AI, health care AI, or LLM agents, you can join our sponsored projects. You’ll also mentor students and write proposals, which prepares you for a faculty career.

    Email any time.

  • Summer research interns

    We look for students who can lead a summer project on their own. We contact candidates for Zoom interviews before May.

    Apply through the form by April 1.

  • Spring and fall research interns

    We contact candidates within a month of the deadline.

    Apply through the form by December 1 for spring, or August 1 for fall.

The form sends us an email automatically, so there’s no need to email separately. If the form doesn’t work for you, send your materials to d.wang@northeastern.edu.
